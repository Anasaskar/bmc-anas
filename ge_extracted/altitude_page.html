Lives truly change when the advanced becomes the ubiquitous

The all-new Revolution™ Altitude is an approachable, customizable introduction to our advanced CT platform. It’s been thoughtfully configured for high image quality, increased efficiency and greater clinical capability for those looking to elevate their care day in and day out.

Key features
Rise to a new level of care
Technology advancements
Elevated performance for your practice

Revolution Altitude’s imaging chain introduces a new generation of performance, with purpose-built hardware and advanced reconstruction working seamlessly together. At its core, the 40 mm Lumia detector captures fine image details, delivering up to 0.25 mm spatial resolution1, strong low-contrast detectability, and improved noise reduction performance.

 

Paired with the scalable Performix™ 2.0 tube, offering up to7 MHU capacity and up to 0.35 second rotation speed, every component is engineered to work in synergy toward one goal:giving clinicians sharper insights even with higher throughput.

Workflow intelligence
Higher standard in efficiency

Revolution Altitude streamlines the CT experience from positioning to image delivery. Our Smart Workflow solution combines a gantry mounted tablet with the AI powered Xtream Camera to automate patient centering and protocol selection, reducing clicks by up to 80%2 and positioning time by 56%3. By automatically aligning the patient to isocenter, it helps reduce variability that can increase dose and image noise.

 

Combined with Auto Views, for automated & AI powered post processing reviews, Revolution Altitude helps keep workflows moving efficiently while supporting consistent image quality and dose management.

Built for today and tomorrow
Greater access above all
Premium technology, made more accessible. Designed with scalable performance and flexible configurations, Revolution Altitude adapts to your clinical and operational needs. Choosing the technology you need today means investing in a platform built to grow with you tomorrow.
Deep learning
Advancing deep learning to new heights

TrueFidelity™ DL is an advanced AI-based image reconstruction technology designed to deliver high-definition CT images with low noise and natural image texture. By leveraging a Deep Neural Network (DNN) trained on carefully selected data, it supports consistent image quality across a wide range of clinical applications.

 

TrueFidelity DL also delivers dose reduction potential by up to 85%⁴, and can also improve low contrast detectability, noise reduction and contrast noise ratio.

Sustainability
A lighter footprint by design

Designed with environmental responsibility in mind, Revolution Altitude features a new generation detector and optimized gantry thermal management to reduce power consumption and improve energy efficiency. The new Energy Saving Mode also achieves an annual reduction of up to 47% (in kWh) according to the COCIR SRI 2015 testing procedure with 260 working days per year.

 

With more than 88% of materials recyclable5 and a scalable, upgradeable design that supports long term system life, sustainability is built into every level of the platform.
